# Business rules

- One business, one configured two-decimal ledger currency, one inventory pool. Currency cannot change after even a draft document exists; deliberate migration is required.
- Product SKU is trimmed, uppercased and unique, including archives. Names/notes/identifiers have explicit length limits. Product forms never directly change stock or average cost. Historical products/partners are archived, never deleted.
- Category/unit deletion fails when referenced by any product. SaleStaff can search products but cannot read purchase costs. Supplier PII is unavailable to SalesStaff, customer PII unavailable to InventoryStaff.
- Purchase drafts require an active supplier, 1–100 distinct active products and positive quantities. They do not alter stock. Full receiving occurs once from Draft; it commits balances, effective receipt costs, movements, status and audit atomically. Received purchases cannot be cancelled or silently edited. Partial receiving/vendor returns are future work.
- Sales allow walk-in customers or an active customer. Client prices/costs/totals are ignored. Draft prices come from the database; confirmation reloads current prices/stock. A price change rejects confirmation with 409 and requires cancelling/recreating the draft for review. Server recalculates all totals. Product snapshots remain historical.
- State labels: Purchase Completed is displayed as Received; Sale Completed is confirmed. Draft may transition to Completed or Cancelled. Sale Completed may transition to Reversed for a **full physical return**. Reversed/Cancelled cannot be reprocessed.
- Negative stock is prohibited. Stock/cost/movements use the shared gate. Authorized manual increases require receipt unit cost; decreases retain average cost and represent counted stock loss. No silent balance edits.
- UUID invoice numbers include UTC creation date and the document ID, protected by a unique SQL index. They are unique, not gapless/sequential fiscal invoice numbers; jurisdictions requiring approved numbering need additional implementation before use.
- Payment records use Cash, Card, BankTransfer or MobileFinancialService as labels, without processing a real payment gateway. Positive two-decimal receipts cannot exceed original invoice total less prior receipts. Refunds require Admin/Manager, reason and cannot exceed collected funds remaining.
- Refund-only entries return money but do not restore physical goods. Partial monetary refunds are supported. They represent credits against already-paid consideration; they do not reopen refunded debt. Outstanding remains original total less collected payments for Completed invoices.
- Full physical sale reversal restores every line once at its captured historical cost, voids the remaining receivable, and preserves original invoice/payment records. Archived returned products are reactivated with audit. Refund due is collected funds less refunds and must be recorded separately. Reversal never silently refunds money. Partial physical returns and standalone stockless voids of completed sales are not offered.
- Operation UUID keys must remain stable across retries. Exact request replay returns the original result; a reused key with different request content conflicts. A new key cannot bypass processed-document state or refundable-balance checks.
- All important catalog/stock/document/settlement/user changes create safe audit entries. Stock movements, processed documents, settlement records and audit history have no edit/delete endpoints.
