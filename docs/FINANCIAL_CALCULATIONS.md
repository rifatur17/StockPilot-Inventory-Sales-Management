# Financial calculations

Decimal arithmetic is used throughout. Money rounds to two decimals using MidpointRounding.AwayFromZero; quantities accept at most three decimals, moving cost six. Nonnegative price/amount limit is 999999999; a line quantity is positive and at most 1000000. Stock balances cannot exceed 999999999. No browser-provided price, computed total, balance or payment state is authoritative.

For each line: `lineTotal = round(quantity × unitPrice, 2)`. Document subtotal is the sum of rounded lines. Document discount is an absolute amount between zero and subtotal. Tax rate is 0–100 with two decimals. Tax is `round((subtotal - discount) × rate / 100, 2)`. Total is `subtotal - discount + tax`. All document totals are validated within the supported money limit.

Moving weighted-average unit cost on a receipt/increase is `round((oldStock × oldAverage + receivedQuantity × effectiveUnitCost)/(oldStock + receivedQuantity), 6)`. Purchase effective unit cost allocates its document discount proportionally: `round(unitPrice × (1 - discount/subtotal), 6)`; for a zero subtotal it is unitPrice (zero). Purchase tax is treated as recoverable and excluded from stock valuation. Nonrecoverable tax/freight require a deliberate future landed-cost design. An adjustment increase explicitly supplies cost. Removing stock does not recalculate its remaining unit average.

Sale confirmation captures current average unit cost for every line. Historic COGS is the sum of `round(quantity × capturedUnitCost,2)`. Later receipts or product edits cannot alter historic costs. A full physical return adds its quantity using the captured unit cost in the current weighted-average calculation.

Reports select documents by UTC completion date and **current** state; they are cohort summaries, not as-of statements or refund-event-date ledgers. Draft/Cancelled are excluded; Reversed sales are excluded from revenue/COGS. Gross revenue excludes sales tax and deducts document discounts. For a monetary refund on a nonreversed sale, revenue credit is proportional to its pretax share: `round(refundAmount × (subtotal-discount)/total,2)`; zero total produces zero credit. Net revenue subtracts these credits. COGS stays incurred when money is refunded without physical goods. Gross profit estimate is net revenue minus captured COGS. It excludes operating expenses, adjustments and other accounting allocations.

The Cash refunded card includes all monetary refunds for the selected completion cohort, including reversed invoices; it is not subtracted twice from excluded reversed revenue. Payment method totals show signed collected funds/refunds for this cohort. Product/category groups allocate discounts per line before monetary credits, so rounding and credits can differ from net summary cards. Staff/daily groups show net revenue. Best sellers rank quantities for nonreversed Completed sales. Purchase totals include purchase tax; current stock valuation is current quantity × average cost, rounded at the summary.

Payment status derives from immutable receipts/refunds. Draft/Cancelled documents are NotPosted; a zero-value Completed invoice is Paid without creating a payment record. Completed: Unpaid/PartiallyPaid/Paid, or Refunded when any monetary credit exists; balances are also shown. Reversed: RefundDue while collected-minus-refunded is positive, otherwise Reversed. Refunds never reopen a customer's debt. Values across different businesses/currencies must never be combined.
